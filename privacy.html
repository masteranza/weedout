Privacy Policy

Effective: August 22, 2026

Weedout for YouTube does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

No data collection

The extension has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no third-party services. The App Store privacy label for this app is “Data Not Collected.”

All processing happens on your device

To decide whether a video carries YouTube’s “Made with AI” label, the extension requests that video’s public metadata directly from youtube.com — the same kind of request your browser already makes when you browse YouTube. These requests go only to YouTube and contain only the public video ID.

Local cache only

Filter verdicts (“labeled AI” / “not labeled”) and your settings are stored locally in Safari’s extension storage on your Mac. They never leave your device and can be erased at any time with the popup’s “Reset cache” button or by removing the extension.

No browsing history

The extension runs only on youtube.com and does not read, record, or transmit your browsing activity.
